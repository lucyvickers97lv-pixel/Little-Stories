How it works

From photo to portrait

You'll see your portrait take shape from the first sketch to the final details, and nothing ships until you're happy with it.

Get in touch

Send an enquiry with any questions. I'll get back to you before anything is ordered.

Place your order

Pick your size and number of pets, and I'll confirm timings and next steps.

Send your photos

Email 1–3 clear reference photos with your order reference. The better the photo, the more detail I can capture.

Tell me the details

A favourite collar, a wonky ear, that particular look: let me know what matters most to you.

Watch it come to life

Once I start drawing, I'll share progress photos and videos along the way.

Approve & receive

You'll get a photo of the finished portrait to approve before it's carefully packed and posted.

Photo tips

Getting the best reference photos

  1. At eye levelGet down to your pet's height rather than shooting from above.
  2. Natural lightNear a window or outdoors, avoiding flash and harsh shadows.
  3. Sharp eyesMake sure the eyes are in focus; they carry the personality.

About the artist

Hi, I'm Lucy.

I'm the artist behind Pawtrait Studio by LRT, drawing pet portraits from my studio in Liverpool. Our pets are family, and I love creating something that captures who they really are: the expression, the fur, the little quirks only their people notice.

Every portrait is drawn entirely by hand, layer by layer, from your own photographs. Whether it's a gift, a celebration of a new arrival, or a memorial for a pet who's no longer with you, I treat each commission with the same care.

Let's draw your pet

Tell me a little about your pet and the portrait you'd like. I'll reply personally to confirm availability, timings and how to pay.

Do portraits come framed?

They're mounted but not framed, so you can choose a frame that suits your home.

How long will it take?

Around 2–3 weeks of drawing once started. The wait before that depends on the queue, and I'll tell you when I accept your order. If it's a gift for a date, mention it!

Can you draw a pet who has passed away?

Of course. Memorial portraits are some of the most meaningful pieces I draw. Send the best photos you have and we'll work from those together.

Can I return a portrait?

As each portrait is custom made, returns aren't accepted, but you'll approve a photo of the finished piece before it ships, and if there's ever a problem, please get in touch.

Where do you ship?

I ship from Liverpool across the UK. For anywhere else, send a message and I'll check options.
